# 01. Pure Sound-Based Mechanical Amplifier

## Overview
This module explores purely mechanical signal amplification through physical acoustic impedance matching. By translating low-displacement/high-pressure acoustic inputs into high-displacement acoustic outputs without electrical conversion, the system achieves physical gain through mechanical advantage.

## Key Principles
* **Acoustic Impedance Matching:** Dual-diaphragm mechanical transformation ratios ($A_1 / A_2$).
* **Friction Limits:** Identifies mechanical boundary friction and mass damping thresholds in solid-linkage drivers.
* **Bandwidth:** Effective across low-to-mid range frequencies ($50\text{ Hz} - 2.5\text{ kHz}$).
