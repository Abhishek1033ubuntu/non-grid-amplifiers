# 02. Permanent Magnetic Amplification Stage

## Overview
Investigation of non-contact magnetic force modulation using permanent magnetic circuits. By shaping magnetic flux lines into extreme gradients ($dB/dx$), mechanical movement on a primary diaphragm modulates secondary magnetic forces across a closed boundary with zero mechanical wear.

## Technical Specifications
* **Magnet Material:** Grade N52 Neodymium ($B_r \approx 1.45\text{ Tesla}$).
* **Pole Piece Material:** Permendur 49 ($49\%\text{ Fe}, 49\%\text{ Co}, 2\%\text{ V}$), offering a high magnetic saturation limit of $\approx 2.4\text{ Tesla}$.
* **Flux Gradient Target:** $> 0.85\text{ T/mm}$ across a $1.0\text{ mm}$ wall gap.
